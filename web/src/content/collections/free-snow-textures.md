Snow turns a plain landscape into a winter scene: mountain terrain, village streets, forest clearings and holiday renders. A good snow material is bright but not pure white, with soft drifts, small bumps and a slight sparkle from the ice crystals.

This page searches the texture sites at once and lists free snow and ice materials, refreshed automatically. Each card shows its source and licence; most are CC0, but check before shipping because not every source uses CC0.

## What to look for

- **Normal and height maps.** Drifts, footprints and crust need depth. Use the height map for parallax or tessellation on close-up ground.
- **Colour.** Avoid pure white in the colour map. A slightly blue-grey base keeps detail visible under bright light.
- **Photographic or stylised.** Several sources carry hand-painted snow for low-poly and stylised games; pick by the look of your scene.
- **Resolution.** 1K or 2K is enough for game terrain and web scenes. Go to 4K or 8K for close-ups.

Pair snow with the grass, stone and sand collections for terrain blending, and with an overcast or forest HDRI for lighting.
