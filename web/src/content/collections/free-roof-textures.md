Roofs cover a large part of any building seen from above or at a distance, so a good roof material matters in architectural scenes, city builders and game levels. Typical surfaces are clay tiles, slate, wooden shingles, thatch, bitumen shingles and corrugated sheet.

This page gathers free roof materials from several texture sites with one live search, refreshed automatically. Each card shows its source and licence; most are CC0.

## Choosing a roof material

- **Scale.** Roof tiles are small, so check how many tiles a single texture covers. Slightly too large a pattern is obvious from a distance.
- **Orientation.** The rows of tiles should run horizontally across the slope. Rotate the UVs if they run the wrong way.
- **Depth.** Tiles and shingles overlap, so displacement or a strong normal map adds a lot of realism.
- **Weathering.** Moss, stains and fading make older roofs convincing; clean variants suit new builds.

Use the same normal map convention as your engine: OpenGL for Blender, Three.js, Godot and Unity, DirectX for Unreal.
