Houses and buildings fill the world around the player: village cottages, suburban homes, shops, towers and whole city blocks. Low-poly versions suit mobile games, prototypes and web scenes, while detailed models are better for architectural renders and close-up cameras.

This page searches the model sites at once and lists free house and building models, refreshed automatically. Each card shows its source, formats and licence.

## What to look for

- **Exterior only or with an interior.** Many game models are shells with no inside. If the player enters, check for an interior or build it from separate rooms.
- **Modular parts.** Kits with walls, roofs and doors let you build many different buildings from a few pieces.
- **Scale and pivot.** Check that the model is in metres and that the pivot is at ground level, so it sits on terrain without adjustment.
- **Textures.** Atlas or vertex-coloured models are light and consistent. Separate PBR materials look better up close but cost more memory.
- **Format.** Use glTF or GLB for Three.js, Babylon.js and Godot, and FBX for Unity and Unreal Engine.
