Animals bring a scene to life: sheep on a hillside, birds in a forest, fish in a pond or a dog beside a cottage. Free animal models range from tiny low-poly assets built for mobile games to detailed sculpts and scans made for close-up rendering.

This page gathers animal models from the model libraries searched at once, so you can compare styles and licences in one place. Each card shows its source and licence; filter for CC0 if you want to use a model without credit.

## What to check before you download

- **Polygon count.** Low-poly animals suit mobile and web projects; high-poly sculpts are better for stills and need retopology for games.
- **Rig and animation.** An animated creature saves hours, but check the clips included and whether the skeleton suits your engine.
- **Format.** glTF/GLB is the easiest route into Three.js and most engines; FBX and OBJ are widely supported by Blender and other tools.
- **Scale and pivot.** Animal models vary in size, so set a sensible real-world scale and put the pivot at the feet before you place many copies.
- **Licence.** CC0 needs no credit; CC-BY requires attribution; some licences forbid commercial use.

In Blender you can pose a static model with an armature, then export it as glTF for the web.
