Swords are among the most reused props in games. Free sword models range from chunky low-poly fantasy blades to katanas, sabres and historically inspired pieces, ready to place in a scene or attach to a character.

This page gathers them from several sites with one live search, refreshed automatically. Each card shows its source, licence and formats.

## What to look for

- **Pivot and scale.** The pivot should be at the grip and the scale in metres, so the sword attaches to a hand bone without adjustment.
- **Style match.** Swords from the same pack share colours and level of detail with that pack's characters and environments.
- **Formats.** glTF or GLB drop straight into web engines and Godot; FBX suits Unity and Unreal.
- **Licence.** Check whether credit is required before you ship.

Combine them with characters and medieval or dungeon kits from the related collections.
