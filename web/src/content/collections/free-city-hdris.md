City HDRIs are panoramic photographs of streets, squares and urban corners, used to light a 3D scene and to fill its background. They suit architecture visualisation, vehicle shots, characters in an urban setting and any render that needs the colour and reflections of a real town.

This page searches the HDRI sites at once and lists free city and street environment maps, refreshed automatically. Each card shows its source and licence; most are CC0, but check before shipping because not every source uses CC0.

## What to look for

- **Time of day.** Overcast streets give soft, even light. Evening and night scenes give coloured light from windows and lamps.
- **Dynamic range.** Streets with direct sun hold very bright and dark areas. Choose files that keep the full range so the sun casts proper shadows.
- **Resolution.** 2K for lighting, 4K or more when the HDRI is visible as a backdrop.
- **Format.** Blender and Three.js load HDR or EXR directly. In Unreal, import the HDR file as a texture cube.

For a clean product or car look, compare these with the studio and overcast collections.
