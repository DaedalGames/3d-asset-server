A sky HDRI is a panorama of an open sky that lights a 3D scene and also serves as its background. Clear blue skies give crisp shadows and a bright, neutral light that suits product shots, architecture, vehicles and game levels set in daytime.

This page searches several HDRI sites at once and lists free sky panoramas, refreshed automatically. Each card shows its source, licence and available resolutions. Most are CC0, so they can be used in commercial work without credit.

## Choosing a sky

- **Sun or no sun.** A visible sun gives hard shadows and a clear light direction. A sky with soft cloud gives gentler shadows. Choose by the mood you want.
- **Ground and horizon.** Some panoramas show buildings or trees at the horizon. For a plain backdrop, look for open fields or a clean horizon, or blur the lower half.
- **Format.** HDR files load in Three.js with RGBELoader and in Blender, Unity and Godot. EXR keeps more precision and suits Unreal Engine and offline renders.
- **Size.** Use the smallest resolution that looks sharp as a background.

For low sun, try the sunset collection. For soft, shadowless light, use the overcast collection.
