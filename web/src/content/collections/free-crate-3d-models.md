Crates and boxes are among the most common props in games: they fill warehouses, docks, dungeons, villages and sci-fi corridors, and they give cover and loot in shooters and RPGs. Free versions range from a few polygons to detailed PBR models, in wood, metal and plastic.

This page gathers free crate models from several sites with one live search, refreshed automatically. Each card shows its source, licence and formats.

## What to look for

- **Polygon budget.** Low-poly crates suit mobile and web games. Detailed models suit close-up renders.
- **Variations.** Packs with open, closed, broken and stacked variants break up repetition.
- **Scale and pivot.** Check that the model is in metres and the pivot sits at the base, so it drops onto the floor.
- **Textures.** Models with a single shared texture atlas are cheapest to draw. Pair plain models with wood textures from the related collections.

Check the licence of each card before you ship. CC0 models need no credit, while other licences may require attribution.
